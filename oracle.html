Adam / God

The Oracle

The holiest feature in TempleOS is a stopwatch. You watch numbers fly, you press a key to latch one, and the machine answers with words from a 7,569-word vocabulary — his vocabulary, in his file, picked by his formula.

This is presented as his artifact, not a toy. The timer here runs on your browser clock, not his keyboard timer, and the random bits come from your machine — everything else is verbatim.
