Every ISO

Every release he ever shipped, from HOPPY in 2004 to the last TempleOS build in 2017 — plus the 5.03 snapshots, the supplemental discs, and the tools. All of it, none of it held back.

How to run one

It is 64-bit, and it matters

TempleOS and its predecessors need a 64-bit machine. QEMU works, and so does VMware, and that is where the community still runs it.

He asked for 512 MB of RAM as a minimum. He was not exaggerating.

Check the hash

Every download here has a SHA-256, taken from the checksums published alongside the originals. Copy the hex, run it through any hash tool, and you have proof you are running the bytes he shipped and not a corrupted copy.
